# Phase A concept comparison

Scores are relative exploration judgments, not approval decisions.

| Criterion | A — Route Beacon | B — Night Dispatch | C — Field Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Originality | High — route spine and mineral identity | High — live operations-room composition | High — editorial dispatch ledger |
| Operational readability | High | High in controlled lighting | Very high for lists/audit detail |
| Information density | Balanced | High and spatial | Highest and row-oriented |
| Arabic quality | Very high; warm, generous hierarchy | High; compact telemetry needs careful copy | Very high; Arabic rows feel native |
| Mobile suitability | Very high; task-first | High; cockpit is clear but visually intense | High; excellent facts, less map-emotional |
| Accessibility risk | Low–medium | Medium–high due deep surfaces | Low–medium |
| Implementation risk | Low–medium | Medium–high | Medium |
| Future flexibility | High | High if dark identity is maintained consistently | Very high for data-heavy modules |

## Recommendation — not a decision

**Recommend Concept A, Route Beacon.** It creates the clearest original product
signature without sacrificing Arabic readability, map prominence, daily
information density, or migration safety. It can scale from Owner command
center to Driver actions while keeping the existing light MapLibre ecosystem
and operational dialogs practical.

Choose Concept B if a dedicated 24/7 control-room identity is the priority and
the higher contrast-audit cost is acceptable. Choose Concept C if auditable
density, tables, and dispatch-record clarity outweigh a more spatial/map-led
brand expression.

No concept has been selected or merged. Phase B remains blocked until the user
explicitly approves one direction and any requested corrections.
